# A2 Unit 1 language-depth completion

Updated 2026-10-10.

Unit 1 (`a2-recent-activities`) is the first A2 unit where **all five lessons** meet DutchFlow's structured language-rich lesson standard.

## Lessons covered

1. `a2-yesterday-and-today`
2. `a2-a-day-at-work`
3. `a2-a-weekend-visit`
4. `a2-telling-a-short-story`
5. `a2-describing-a-changing-routine`

The fifth lesson already had `languageDepth` from the 40-lesson expansion. This pass adds the same depth to the preceding four lessons.

## Added depth per lesson

Every lesson in the unit now has:

- 4 additional contextual Dutch/Arabic examples;
- 3 high-value collocations;
- 2 natural alternative phrasings with usage notes;
- 2 plausible learner mistakes with corrected Dutch and Arabic explanation.

The depth layer supplements the existing core lesson contract: 8 core phrases, dialogue, grammar-in-use, 4 lesson questions, vocabulary/form notes, listening/writing practice and 2 open production tasks.

## Unit 1 lexical focus

The new material deepens rather than broadens randomly:

- perfect tense with `hebben` / `zijn`;
- inversion after fronted time/sequence expressions;
- everyday chunks such as `boodschappen doen`, `thuis werken`, `iemand om hulp vragen`;
- visit language such as `bij iemand op bezoek gaan`, `weer thuiskomen`;
- narrative sequencing with `eerst`, `daarna`, `vervolgens`, `uiteindelijk`;
- routine/frequency language such as `tegenwoordig`, `nog steeds`, `vaker`, `minder vaak`.

## Guardrails

The extra examples are not added to the controlled audio inventory automatically. Existing phrase IDs and audio requests remain unchanged.

The browser and native tests require all five Unit 1 lessons to keep complete language-depth blocks. Mobile rendering must remain free of horizontal overflow.
