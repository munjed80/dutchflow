Internal review covers all 32 Dutch/Arabic phrase pairs, 16 answer keys, 24 form notes and eight writing models. Formal exchanges consistently use `u`. The grammar deliberately limits productive `omdat` practice to a following reason clause with one finite verb; it does not claim a universal order for multi-verb clusters. Indirect `of` and passive confirmation phrases are contextual support, not a complete subordinate-clause syllabus. Writing allows multiple correct formulations and is not exact-match assessment.

Primary references: [Team Taaladvies — want / omdat](https://www.vlaanderen.be/team-taaladvies/taaladviezen/want-omdat) distinguishes main-clause and subordinate-clause order and notes meaning differences; [Taaladvies — omdat at the beginning of a sentence](https://taaladvies.net/omdat-aan-het-begin-van-de-zin/) supports contextual spoken replies. Examples here are original. This internal pass is not external educator sign-off.

## Independent supporting sources

- **Reading `a2-two-appointment-options`:** Lina's Thursday 11:00 is cancelled because of work. Options are Tuesday 09:30 Centrum and Wednesday 14:00 Noord; both 25 minutes. Centrum is beside the station, Noord farther away with a bus stop at the door. Preference is due before Monday 12:00; an independent confirmation follows. Four questions cover reason, a complete alternative, equal duration and the request/confirmation boundary. Multi-detail evidence contains every needed premise.
- **Integrated written source:** Sam's Tuesday 10:00 Centrum is cancelled. Thursday afternoon at the same location is requested but not yet available/confirmed. The response is due by Wednesday 16:00. No exact new appointment time has been granted.
- **Connected listening `a2-passage-appointments-followup`:** the appointment becomes confirmed for Thursday 15:00 Noord, explicitly not Centrum. Meeting in room 4; reception first at 14:50. If unsuitable, call today, explicitly Wednesday, before 17:00. The passage remains within the 65-word source limit. Questions distinguish start time, confirmed location and early arrival; the written response deadline is not reused as a meeting time.
- **Scenario `a2-changing-an-intake-appointment`:** Noor cannot attend Monday 09:00 because of work and has class Tuesday. She chooses Wednesday 15:00 Noord over Tuesday 11:00 Centrum, requests confirmation while staff checks, then restates 15:00 start / 14:50 reception. Premature certainty before the staff confirmation is rejected. Transfer changes to Thursday 10:00 conflict and a request for Friday 13:00 Centrum.
- **Production:** integrated writing uses Monday 09:00/class conflict and Wednesday 14:00 Noord as a requested alternative. Partner practice asks the learner to respond to the partner's actual two offers, give a reason, wait for confirmation and distinguish arrival from start. Models satisfy the requested details without claiming to book a real service.

All ten new comprehension IDs have scoped A2 remediation. State/response-deadline mistakes link to follow-up; confirmed-location/arrival mistakes link to replacement confirmation. All context is fictional, and no institutional booking policy is presented as a universal rule.
