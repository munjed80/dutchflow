## Changes

- Introduce pronouns after personal details, numbers before age/contact details, and months before calendar dates.
- Introduce food and preferences before ordering, colours before clothing sizes, and route practice before public transport.
- Introduce family vocabulary before school messages, occupations before work instructions, and body vocabulary before health appointments.
- Keep four module groups and the first two lessons stable. Roadmap links follow the same relative lesson order.
- Qualify the units-before-tens rule to exclude round tens; distinguish yes/no questions from questions beginning with a question word.
- Explain clause coordination without implying that every `en` or `maar` starts a new clause. Match the translation of `ik werk om acht uur` to the actual sentence.
- Explain `een halve kilo` / `een half brood` through de/het noun patterns, clarify possessive versus verbal `zijn`, and name the reflexive pronoun in `Hoe voel je je?`.
- Replace fabricated distractors in five vocabulary/meaning questions with meaningful Dutch alternatives. Inflection exercises can still contrast correct and incorrect forms deliberately.
- Make the weather question's Arabic translation more natural.
