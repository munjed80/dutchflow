## Supporting tasks

- **Reading `a2-a-museum-trip-plan`:** Sunday meet 09:10/hall; train 09:25–10:05; ten-minute walk gives museum arrival 10:15 before 10:30 tour. Evidence includes necessary premises.
- **Integrated written source:** Wednesday meet 12:10/entrance; train 12:20/platform 3 to fictional Stad-West 12:50; ten-minute walk, centre 13:00, workshop 13:15. Laptop supplied, not required. Accessible table explicitly represents the original plan.
- **Listening `a2-passage-travel-plans`:** original train cancelled; replacement bus 12:30 in front of station; meet 12:20/bus stop. Planned arrival 13:05 plus ten-minute walk means workshop arrival 13:15, exactly its unchanged start, with no buffer. All three questions require the update. The source is within the 65-word limit.
- **Scenario `a2-choosing-a-route-after-cancellation`:** 18:00 train cancelled. Need community centre before 19:00; five-minute walk from station. Bus 18:10–18:55 plus walk arrives 19:00; train 18:25–18:50 plus walk arrives 18:55. Later departure can mean earlier arrival. Ask platform (4), repeat and check screen. Transfer: delayed bus arrives around 20:10, five-minute walk to library 20:15; ask friend whether they can wait.
- **Integrated production:** propose meeting 09:30/bus stop for 09:40–10:20 bus, replacing 09:15/hall; request acceptance. Partner task chooses 17:50 over 18:10 arrival for before-18:00 requirement, then asks and repeats stop 4/departure 17:15 after partner supplies them.
