## Corrections and rationale

- **Vocabulary:** distinguish `langzamer` (slower) from `wat/iets langzamer` (a little slower), clarify `liever`, reflexive pronouns, `willen` versus `vandaag`, and first-person `Ik neem` versus imperative `Neem`. Explain `spoor` as the numbered track and `perron` as the passenger platform; existing practical Arabic platform references remain understandable in station directions. Avoid assigning the ambiguous `ze samen` price question exclusively to socks.
- **Production fidelity:** a prompt asking for two complete address sentences now has two complete sentences; two-line letter models preserve their line break. The rescheduling letter identifies the old Friday appointment, and the SMS request explicitly asks for appointment confirmation. The repair letter names Thursday afternoon. `draagt een bril` directly matches wearing glasses. Self-review no longer insists that `open` be the last word when `alsjeblieft` follows it. Fictional family tasks and first-person role instructions are explicit.
- **Reading:** the absence note teaches `thuisblijven` from the actual excerpt instead of presenting absent `afwezig` as its headword; email translations specify electronic mail. The park grammar translation now matches the meeting time in the story. The `om ... te` note explains the words between `om` and `te maken`.
- **Scenarios:** correct the Arabic missing-letter explanation, distinguish accepting a proposed place from an already agreed place, and smooth the health transfer sentence. All 40 intended choice indices remain unchanged.
- **Evidence:** seven integrated questions now quote the premises used in their explanations, including both times, quantity plus unit price, bus arrival plus walking time, and lesson start plus arrival. Exact evidence remains a contiguous substring of its source. The cheese question asks what is available instead of treating availability as proof that no more is needed.
- **Interaction:** the family oral model includes both adults and children as requested. Work partner prompts supply both actions before the learner repeats them. Weekend and final-review models respond to the partner's changed time/day, with a confirmation prompt. The travel writing model uses an actual station entrance rather than an unspecified entrance at a bus stop.
- **Translation/instructions:** make the homework-delivery question ask about the delivery medium, align an afternoon greeting and clothing-fit wording, and align the study-session prompt with its practice model.
