The 32 new phrase pairs, all 16 lesson answers, 24 lexical/form notes and eight production models received an internal consistency pass. The explanations keep `er` and `als alles klaar is` as contextual patterns, not complete grammar systems. `want` is practised with main-clause order; subordinate-clause production remains for a later unit.

Primary language reference consulted: [Team Taaladvies — want / omdat](https://www.vlaanderen.be/team-taaladvies/taaladviezen/want-omdat). It distinguishes coordination from subordination and notes that these conjunctions are not interchangeable in every context. Examples in this unit are original. The delivery does not claim independent linguistic sign-off.
