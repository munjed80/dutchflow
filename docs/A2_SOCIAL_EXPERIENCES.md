## Learning scope

The unit closes the proposed sequence with four observable social tasks rather than a generic leisure vocabulary list.

1. `a2-sharing-a-social-experience`: report where the learner went, with whom, what happened and a simple opinion. It recycles the perfect tense from unit 1 and separates event facts from evaluation.
2. `a2-asking-follow-up-questions`: ask distinct questions about place, companion, duration and opinion instead of repeating one known fact.
3. `a2-responding-to-an-invitation`: accept clearly or decline one time with a reason and propose an alternative. It distinguishes refusing the time from refusing the social activity.
4. `a2-confirming-a-social-plan`: distinguish an initial proposal from the accepted plan and restate the final day, time, place and preparation after a change.

The Dutch examples deliberately stay within simple everyday interaction. `want` and guided `omdat` remain supported patterns; the material does not attempt a full subordinate-clause syllabus.

## Supporting tasks

- **Reading `a2-an-invitation-to-a-neighbourhood-evening`:** distinguishes door-opening time from activity start, requested cup from provided food, Wednesday reply deadline from Saturday event time, and a pending room confirmation from a known final room.
- **Scenario `a2-changing-a-social-invitation`:** the learner keeps the Sunday park invitation, rejects only the unavailable 14:00 time, gives a family-visit reason, proposes 15:00, accepts the main entrance, asks what to bring and finally repeats the accepted plan.
- **Integrated written source:** a Saturday game evening originally starts 19:30; Samir provides tea/food and asks Karim to bring two glasses. A possible 20:00 start is not yet final.
- **Integrated listening update:** the event now starts 20:00, Noor arrives later, Fatima no longer comes, Lina does, the glass request is cancelled, and Karim is asked to arrive at the front door at 19:50. Questions require using the update rather than retaining the original message.
- **Writing transfer:** decline only an 18:00 dinner time because of work, propose 19:15 and ask for acceptance.
- **Partner task:** tell a short past experience, answer three follow-up questions and move from that past event to a new joint proposal.

All ten comprehension questions have scoped remediation links. Production remains ungraded and accepts alternative valid Dutch.
