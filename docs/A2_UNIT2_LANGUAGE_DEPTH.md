## Language added

The unit now develops practical workplace Dutch through:

- progress reporting: `bijna klaar zijn met`, `een taak afronden`, `op de lijst staan`;
- delay language: `vertraging oplopen`, `een tijd niet redden`, `iets alvast doorsturen`;
- clarification: `om verduidelijking vragen`, `Als ik het goed begrijp...`, `klopt dat?`;
- handover/responsibility: `een taak op zich nemen`, `een overdracht bevestigen`, `klaarstaan voor vervoer`;
- priorities and parallel work from the fifth lesson.

The alternatives deliberately contrast useful structures such as `want` versus `omdat`, and short practical phrasing versus more formal workplace Dutch.

## Guardrails

No core phrase IDs, audio request texts, lesson counts, progress semantics or scoring behaviour change.

The additional language-depth examples remain reading/enrichment material and are not automatically added to the controlled audio inventory.
