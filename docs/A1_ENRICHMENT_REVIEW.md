## Findings and corrections

- Seventeen new integrated questions repeated an existing comprehension objective. Replaced them with distinct source-supported details, action/negation interpretation, or a new price calculation. Kept question IDs and answer indices; updated lesson remediation for revised objectives. This also removes the contradictory required-field explanation and the question that incorrectly attributed the invitation to its recipient Sam.
- Clarified that the transport waiting-time question refers to the first bus, before walking. The `half twee` question now answers one-thirty without inferring AM/PM from an audio passage that does not specify it.
- Four lesson models now explicitly distinguish Thursday practice, teacher/parent roles, an SMS confirmation request and a practical demonstration (`voordoen`).
- Six integrated writing tasks now clarify audience, requested details, natural appointment requests and whether the final task uses the original library message or its later cancellation.
- Eight second-round oral models now respond to partner cues, correct misunderstandings, demonstrate the requested activity or negotiate the changed appointment. Shopping stays within one fictional shop interaction.
- Seven new reading records receive targeted refinements: contextual `als`, a hidden character in `schrijven`, explicit absence-of-class wording, parent-meeting title, feminine Arabic address, clear unconfirmed appointment language, bounded evidence claims, and a complete weather-condition quotation.
- The practice validator now rejects identical normalized prompts within one activity. It cannot detect semantic duplication or prove an answer follows from a passage; editorial review remains necessary.
- Corrected handoff sections that attributed the merged batch to nonexistent PRs #22/#23 and carried mutually inconsistent counts.
