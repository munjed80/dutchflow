# A2 preparation outline

This document prepares the next stage after the current A1 expansion. It is a planning artifact only. No A2 learner routes, paid assessments, certificates, or hidden proficiency claims ship from this file.

## Purpose

Use A2 planning to keep future content coherent while A1 is still being completed. A2 should extend the same practical, Arabic-first teaching model: everyday situations, short explanations, contextual reading/listening, and original-response practice without presenting unfinished content as an official level.

## Entry requirement

Do not label new learner content as A2 until the A1 map has deeper coverage across reading, listening, guided writing, and spoken interaction. The readiness gates in `docs/A1_CURRICULUM.md` still take priority over adding new level labels.

## Proposed communicative units

1. **Daily coordination and work routines**
   - clarify tasks, sequence steps, report delay, ask a colleague for help
2. **Housing and local services**
   - describe a recurring housing problem, compare options, follow service instructions
3. **Appointments, changes, and follow-up**
   - move an appointment, explain the reason, confirm new details, ask what happens next
4. **School and formal communication**
   - read a longer school or municipal message, answer with a short functional reply
5. **Travel, social plans, and free time**
   - compare transport options, react to weather-dependent plans, invite or decline with a reason

## Content expectations for future A2 work

- **Lessons:** longer micro-dialogues with a clearer before/during/after task flow
- **Reading:** longer connected texts with both explicit information and simple inference
- **Listening:** new recordings with unfamiliar details, not phrase-only playback
- **Writing:** short functional messages, form-like replies, and guided comparisons between options
- **Speaking preparation:** role-play briefs and observable success criteria, even if full audio scoring is not implemented

## Readiness gates before shipping A2 content

1. A Dutch/Arabic language editor reviews the wording, ordering, and distractors.
2. Each A2 unit includes at least one unseen reading and one unseen listening task.
3. Each A2 unit includes one short original writing task and one oral interaction prompt.
4. New vocabulary and structures recycle across later units instead of appearing once.
5. No A2 score, certificate, or paid exam claim appears without separate reviewed assessment design.

## Technical boundaries

- Keep regular lessons free.
- Do not reuse the free placement endpoint as A2 assessment or paid-exam authorization.
- Preserve stable lesson slugs and phrase IDs once published.
- Update `README.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/A1_CURRICULUM.md`, and the roadmap data when any planned A2 work becomes real learner content.
