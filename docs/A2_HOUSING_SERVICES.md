## Supporting resources: keep the facts distinct

- **Library reading `a2-a-repair-confirmation`:** Sara's kitchen tap was repaired Tuesday; the bedroom window still closes poorly. The cancelled Thursday 09:00–11:00 appointment is replaced by Friday 13:00–15:00. Email confirmation is due before Wednesday 16:00. Four questions cover purpose, direction of the room comparison, current visit window and response deadline; quoted evidence includes all premises needed for each answer.
- **Integrated written source:** Omar's bedroom door has stuck since Saturday. The window was repaired last week. Friday 10:00–12:00 is initially booked; the broken doorbell means a phone call on arrival. Email confirmation is initially due before Thursday 16:00.
- **Integrated spoken update `a2-passage-housing-services`:** the day stays Friday but the window becomes 13:00–15:00. The doorbell has now been repaired, so the technician rings it. Email confirmation is due today, explicitly Thursday, before 18:00. The short passage stays within the existing 65-word audio contract. Three questions target the updated window, contact method and response deadline rather than copying the written answers.
- **Scenario `a2-calling-about-a-repair`:** Noor's kitchen tap was repaired Thursday and leaked again from Saturday. Tuesday morning is unavailable; she requests Friday 13:00–15:00. The partner accepts it, after which she confirms the whole window. Saying “exactly 13:00” is explicitly rejected. Transfer changes the problem to a bedroom window and requests Thursday 14:00–16:00.
- **Production:** integrated writing changes the tap location, repair/onset days and requested window. Partner practice changes to a kitchen window, rejects Tuesday morning and confirms the partner's offered Thursday 15:00–17:00 window, including availability and an email-confirmation request. Models demonstrate these requested details; prompts use fictional situations and never book real services.

All ten new comprehension questions have scoped A2 remediation. These are language exercises: no home-repair instructions, technical diagnosis, rental-rights advice or real appointment promises are introduced.
