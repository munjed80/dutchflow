# A2 Dutch/Arabic editorial consistency pass

Updated 2026-10-08. This pass reviews the authored A2 course as one published sequence after the cumulative final review and targeted mediation reinforcement.

## Scope reviewed

The editorial review covered:

- 32 A2 lessons;
- 256 lesson phrases and their Arabic translations/tips;
- 192 lexical/form notes;
- 64 lesson production tasks;
- 8 reading texts and 32 reading questions;
- 8 guided scenarios;
- 9 integrated practice packs, including the cumulative final review.

The purpose was not to rewrite stylistically sound material. Changes were limited to wording that could teach an ambiguous distinction, sound less natural in ordinary Dutch, or weaken the match between the Dutch model and the Arabic instruction.

## Corrections made

### Registration deadline versus event time

The school/local-messages speaking model previously used:

`Ik meld twee personen aan voor vrijdag vier uur.`

Although interpretable, that structure can sound as if Friday at 16:00 belongs to the registration itself as an appointment-like time. The model now separates the participant count from the deadline:

`Ik meld twee personen aan. Ik doe dat voor vrijdag vier uur.`

The Arabic model likewise separates “two people” from “before Friday 16:00”. This reinforces the course-wide distinction between a **deadline** and the **time of the activity**.

### Natural request for a borrowed laptop

The production model:

`Ik gebruik graag een laptop van het buurthuis.`

was changed to:

`Ik wil graag een laptop van het buurthuis gebruiken.`

The revised sentence is a clearer model for expressing a preference/request in this reply context.

### Final-review school location

The final-review update used:

`in school De Horizon, lokaal vijf`

It now uses:

`op school De Horizon, in lokaal vijf`

The same wording is used consistently in the listening evidence, relay-writing model and partner model.

### Formal salutation

`Beste organisatie` was replaced by `Beste medewerker` in the final-review clarification email. The Arabic translation was adjusted accordingly.

### Housing-service reading ambiguity

The written housing message used `voor de deur` where the intended meaning was “for the bedroom door”. Because `voor de deur` strongly invites the location reading “at/in front of the door”, the written source now says:

`Onze monteur komt vrijdag tussen tien en twaalf uur om naar de deur van de slaapkamer te kijken.`

The closing sentence was also clarified from `Het bezoek is op uw eigen adres` to `De monteur komt naar uw eigen adres`.

The earlier housing **listening** source was left unchanged because its established audio request identity is protected and the surrounding context still makes the intended repair target recoverable. No earlier-unit listening source was rewritten in this pass.

## Consistency checks completed

The pass specifically checked:

- `u` versus `je` within a single interaction;
- `want` main-clause order;
- `omdat` subordinate-clause order;
- inversion after `daarom`;
- `moeten`, `hoeven`, and `mogen` distinctions;
- deadline versus appointment/start/arrival time;
- original versus superseded information;
- route arrival versus final-destination arrival;
- optional versus required information;
- model/checklist alignment;
- reading/listening evidence against the keyed answer;
- role, name, location and time continuity.

Mixed formal/informal language across separate activities is retained where the role changes intentionally—for example, a formal organiser notice followed by an informal message to a friend. That is not treated as a consistency defect.

## What was intentionally not changed

- No unit 9 was added.
- No lesson, phrase, reading, scenario or practice-pack count changed.
- No grammar system was expanded beyond the authored A2 scope.
- No stylistic synonym replacements were made where the existing Dutch was already natural and pedagogically clear.
- Earlier unit listening sources were not rewritten merely for stylistic preference.
- No CEFR score, certificate or validation claim was added.
